KNN: recommend from your nearest neighbors

Each dot is a user, placed by taste. We find the 20 closest to you by cosine distance and rank the films they loved that you have not seen.
Decisions and trade-offs
01Cosine, not Euclidean: matches taste, ignores how harshly someone rates.
02k = 20: quality peaks there, then far neighbors add noise.
03argpartition, not sort: top k in O(n).
How it fails Cold start: a new user has a zero vector, so we fall back to most-popular.
Taste space ◆ you ● 20 nearest ● everyone else
Precision at 10 as k grows peak 0.28 at k = 20
